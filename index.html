🍭 Candyfloss Tycoon - Guide

Welcome to Candyfloss Tycoon: Floppy Edition! Manage your seaside candyfloss stall across 7 iconic UK and Korean beaches.

🎮 Gameplay Steps

  • Check the Forecast: Weather affects customer footfall. Heatwaves bring huge crowds, while storms scare visitors away.
  • Spin Bonus Floss: During the 5-second arcade mini-game, swirl your mouse/finger in circles inside the spinning bowl to craft up to +25 bonus candyfloss sticks!
  • Plan Production & Price: Set how many sticks to prepare, set selling prices based on local price sensitivity, and buy advertising signs to attract customers.
  • Bank Loans: Need quick capital? Borrow £50 from the bank, but remember to pay back before 5% daily interest eats your profits!
  • Hotseat Multiplayer: Compete with 1 to 4 players on the same device!
